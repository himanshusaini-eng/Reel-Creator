How it works

From screen recording to finished reel in three steps.

1Choose a devicePick an iPhone, Android, iPad or MacBook frame in graphite, silver or your brand color. Or start from one of the animated templates.
2Upload your videoAdd a screen recording or screenshot. Trim where it starts, set the reel length, choose a motion like float, 3D tilt or orbit, and write a headline.
3ExportRender a 1080×1920 MP4 at 24, 30 or 60 fps, with optional music and fades. Every export stays in the project's history.
