Details

What the studio covers before you export.

Do I need a separate editor after I record?

No. Capture, scenes, captions, brand, and export stay in the same project. The timeline is the cut.

Can one story cover the store and social?

Yes. Reframe the master into 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9, including a silent version for autoplay.

Does AI replace the edit?

It proposes trims, zooms, and joins. You accept what belongs in the story and leave the rest.

Where do templates fit?

A template is a finished story shape. You drop your screens in and keep the pacing, type, and end card.

Can someone else review the cut?

Share a preview from the project, note the scene, and update the same timeline before the final render.
