FAQ

Questions before you start.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel from your account settings. You keep access through the end of the billing period.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free includes core editing, starter templates, and watermarked 720p exports so you can try the studio before upgrading.

Is there a watermark?

Free exports include a Reel Creator watermark. Pro and Enterprise remove it and unlock 1080p exports.

Can I upgrade later?

Anytime. Your projects stay with you — upgrading unlocks longer cuts, the full template library, and cleaner exports.

Do you offer refunds?

If something goes wrong in your first 7 days on Pro, email hello@reelcreator.app and we’ll make it right.

Can teams collaborate?

Team seats and shared brand kits ship with Enterprise. Contact sales if you need multi-seat access today.
